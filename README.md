# Vertigo City

A 3D parkour game that runs in the browser. You play a squirrel scout in a big procedural city: run the rooftops, web-swing between towers, glide on flying-squirrel flaps, ride a hoverboard and grind, fly a plane from the airport, walk into buildings and ride their elevators, turn into a real squirrel to climb walls, trees and ceilings, and rescue people and animals from fires, floods and tornadoes.

## Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. It loads three.js r128 from cdnjs, so it needs an internet connection the first time.

Works with keyboard and mouse, a game controller (X-input layout, e.g. 8BitDo Pro 2 in X mode), or touch. The start screen lists every control.

## What's in it

- A 1.5 km city: Downtown, Midtown, Northside, Harbor Works, Greenway Park, Old Pine Woods and the Kessel River with its bridges
- Parkour: vaulting, wall-running, web swinging, zip line, wingsuit-style gliding
- Hoverboard: carving, kickers, rail and rooftop-edge grinds, flips and combos
- Squirrel form: climb any wall, tree or underside, survive any fall
- A squirrel-sized motorcycle with boost, wheelies and bunny hops
- Surfing on a wave-filled river (and over flooded streets)
- Kessel Field airport with a prop plane you can taxi, take off, fly anywhere and land (or bail out of)
- Enterable buildings: ground-floor lobbies with doors on every side and elevators up to rooftop huts
- Disasters with rescues (no fighting): building fires, river floods, tornadoes
- Traffic, pedestrians, an elevated train you can ride, 20 rings to fly through against the clock
- Cel-shaded hero with elbows and knees, and web-swing animations that alternate paws
- Synthesized sound effects and an original generative synthwave score that follows the action (no audio files)

Everything is in the single `index.html` file.
