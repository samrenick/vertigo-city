VertigoCity

You start on the roof of the tallest tower downtown. You're a squirrel scout. Run the rooftops, then jump and spread your gliding flaps. Fly through all 20 orange rings as fast as you can. They're spread from the Downtown towers to the Kessel River bridges, the Harbor Works docks and the brick rooftops of Northside.
